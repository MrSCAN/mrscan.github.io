About

A quick intro.

I’m a Full-Stack Software Engineer on the MV Enterprise Experience team at Cisco Meraki, where I ship enterprise camera features end-to-end across firmware (C/C++ on Embedded Linux), backend (Ruby on Rails), and dashboard (React).

My work sits at the intersection of embedded systems, computer vision, and on-device ML. I’ve led an embedded License Plate Recognition capability from prototype through General Availability, built ML evaluation frameworks used in production, and helped bring up new camera hardware platforms.

I care deeply about reliability, clean engineering, and customer impact, and enjoy working across the full stack to ship features that make it to real users.
